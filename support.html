Frequently asked questions

How do I add a Pocketly widget?
In Pocketly, open My Widgets, choose a widget, size and colour, then tap Activate widget. Next, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Pocketly. Lock Screen widgets are added by touching and holding the Lock Screen and tapping Customise.

Why hasn't my widget updated yet?
iOS decides when widgets refresh, so updates can take a little while. Opening Pocketly after a change usually refreshes your widgets straight away.

How often do exchange rates update?
Rates come from ExchangeRate-API and update once a day. Pocketly shows when they were last updated and keeps the latest rates for offline use. Rates are indicative only, so check with your bank before a transaction.

What does Pocketly Pro include?
Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. It unlocks unlimited widgets, savings goals, countdowns and favourite currencies, advanced habit stats, premium themes and custom backgrounds. The price is shown in the app.

How do I restore Pocketly Pro?
Open Settings in Pocketly and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone. Pocketly has no account and no servers that receive your data. Deleting the app deletes its data.

How do I turn reminders on or off?
Reminders are optional and are set per feature (Affirmations, Habits, Budget and Countdown). You can turn them off in Pocketly or in iOS Settings → Notifications.
