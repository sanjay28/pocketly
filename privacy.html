Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Pocketly is designed to keep your information on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app stores and the limited information that leaves your device.

Information stored on your device

Savings goals, transactions, expenses, budgets, countdowns, habits, affirmations, favourite quotes, world clocks, currency pairs, widget settings and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Pocketly has no account system and no servers that receive your personal data.

Widgets read a copy of the data they display from a storage area shared only between Pocketly and its own widgets on your device.

Exchange rates

To show current exchange rates, Pocketly downloads a public rate table from a third-party exchange-rate provider (see "Third-party services"). The request contains the base currency code only. Like any internet request, it reveals your device's IP address to the provider. No amounts, balances or other financial records are ever sent.

Purchases

Pocketly Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app checks Apple-signed transaction information on your device to unlock Pro.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone using iOS notifications. Pocketly asks for permission only when you turn on a reminder, and you can turn reminders off at any time in Pocketly or in iOS Settings.

Analytics and tracking

Pocketly contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Third-party services

  • Apple (App Store purchases, notifications, WidgetKit)
  • Exchange-rate provider: ExchangeRate-API (exchangerate-api.com) — receives rate requests as described above.

Deleting your data

Deleting Pocketly removes all of its data from your iPhone. If you use iCloud or device backups, your device backup may contain Pocketly's data under Apple's backup terms.

Children

Pocketly does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app with a new "Last updated" date.
